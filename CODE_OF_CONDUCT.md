# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project (opening issues, reviewing, or
contributing code) agrees to make it a welcoming, respectful place for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people's time and the different ways wards and units operate.
- **Protect member privacy.** Never post real member names, contact details,
  callings, interview notes, or other ward data in issues, pull requests,
  screenshots or logs. Use made-up examples instead.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently
block anyone whose behavior is harmful.

To report a concern privately, contact the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/richebarry-stack). Reports are kept
confidential.
